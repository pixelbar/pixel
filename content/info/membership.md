**Member**: €32.50 per month
24/7 key access (after 2 months), tool use, project display space, a personal shelf, and Spacenet and Pixelbar accounts.

**Friend**: €5 per month or €60 per year
A lighter option if you can't commit to full membership: access when members are present, tool use, and Spacenet and Pixelbar accounts.

**How to join as a member**
1. Visit the space and meet the members.
2. Read the house rules (`/info topic:rules`).
3. Email the membership form to [bestuur@pixelbar.nl](mailto:bestuur@pixelbar.nl).
4. Set up a monthly bank transfer. Membership starts after your first payment.

**Friends** email the board that they'd like to join, with their nickname, and pay by bank transfer.

Full details, the form and the bank details: https://pixelbar.nl/becomingamember/
