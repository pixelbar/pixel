You're welcome to drop by whenever we're open. You don't need to be a member first, and it's a good way to see if Pixelbar is for you.

**Opening times:** Normally Wednesday evening until late, check {{announcements-channel}} and vote in the weekly poll so someone knows you're interested!

**Is it open right now?** Use `/status`, which reads the switch in the space. If it says open but the door is shut, press the doorbell or ask on Discord.

**Good to know**
- You don't need any skills. Beginners are welcome and there's help to learn.
- Under 18 is fine, but an adult must supervise the use of machinery.
- Dutch is spoken most, but it's an international community.
- Sleeping in the space isn't allowed.

More: `/info topic:rules`, `/info topic:membership`
