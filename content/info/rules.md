The short version. The full rules are on the website and are the ones that count (where the English and Dutch versions differ, the Dutch one prevails).

- Respect everyone and everything in the space. You enter at your own risk.
- No sleeping, fire, drugs, smoking or vaping.
- Clean up your things right after you've used them. Anything left lying around becomes available to everyone.
- Non-members can't be left alone in the space. If you bring someone new, you're responsible for them.
- Don't change the infrastructure without the board's permission.
- Nothing that's illegal under Dutch law.
- The board decides who is a member, and can take away access straight away if the rules are broken.

Full rules: https://pixelbar.nl/houserules/
